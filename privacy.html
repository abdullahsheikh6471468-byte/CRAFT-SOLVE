Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share through this website.

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

CraftSolve is a digital agency based in Lahore, Pakistan. This policy explains what happens to the information you give us through this website.

What we collect

The only information this website collects is what you type into the contact form: your name, email address, phone number, company, the service you are interested in, and your project details. Everything is optional except the fields marked as required on the form.

How it is used

When you press the send button, your browser builds a pre-filled WhatsApp message from the details you entered and opens WhatsApp so you can send it to our team. We use the details only to respond to your enquiry and discuss your project.

What this website does not do

This website has no server-side database and does not store your form entries. It does not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or cookies of its own.

Third parties

Messages you send through WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy. Fonts on this website are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers when a page loads. Links to LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook lead to those services, which have their own policies.

Your choices

You can choose not to use the form and contact us directly instead. If you have sent us a message and would like us to delete it, tell us on WhatsApp and we will remove it from our records.

Contact

For any privacy question, message us on WhatsApp or call +92 335 4673607.
